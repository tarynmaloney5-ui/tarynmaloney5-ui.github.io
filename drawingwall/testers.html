Drawing Wall

Become a tester

Android · Free · Takes about a minute

Use Drawing Wall before it launches and help shape it. Testers get the newest features first, and your feedback goes straight to the person building it.

  1. Send us your Google Play email

    Drawing Wall is in a closed test on Google Play, so we add each tester by hand. Email us the Gmail address that's signed in to Google Play on your Android phone.

    Email my request

  2. Watch for our reply

    We'll add you to the test and email you the Google Play link.

  3. Install from Google Play

    Open the link on your phone (or on a computer signed in to the same account), tap Become a tester, then download Drawing Wall from Google Play. Create your account in the app to get started.

Once you're in, see what to test for the features we most need help with.

Please keep the app installed for at least 14 days. Google needs testers to stay on for two weeks before Drawing Wall can launch publicly.

Questions

Is there an iPhone version? Not yet. Drawing Wall is on Android first.

"Become a tester" doesn't show up? Make sure you're signed in to Google Play with the same Gmail address you sent us, then wait a few minutes and try again.

Do I need real drawings? They help most, but new walls start with example drawings, so you can try Story Book straight away.

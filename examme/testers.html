Become a tester

Android · Free · Takes a couple of minutes

Use ExamMe before it launches and help shape it. Testers get new features first, and your feedback goes straight to the person building it.

  1. Send us your Google Play email

    Email us the Gmail address that's signed in to Google Play on your Android phone. We need it to add you to the test.

    Email my request

  2. Wait for your invite

    We'll add your address to the test and email you a link, usually within a day or two.

  3. Install from Google Play

    Open the link on your phone, accept the invite, then download ExamMe from Google Play. Use the same Google account you sent us.

Once you're in, see what to test for the features we most need help with.

Why email? ExamMe is in an early, invite-only test on Google Play, so each tester is added by hand. An easier way to join is coming soon.

Questions

Is there an iPhone version? Not yet. ExamMe is Android only for now.

The invite link says the app isn't available? Make sure you're signed in to Google Play with the address you sent us. If it still doesn't work, email us and we'll check.

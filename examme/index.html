How it works

From "what do you want to learn?" to real progress.

ExamMe keeps studying simple. One box to start, one question at a time, and a clear next step when you finish.

  1. Pick a subjectType anything, from Biology to Spanish to a work course. Add topics or goals if you want to focus on a chapter or a skill.
  2. Read a short lessonEach exam starts with a lesson on the topic, with quick recall questions and common mistakes to watch out for.
  3. Take your examAnswer a mix of multiple choice, true or false and written questions. You see explanations as you go and your results at the end.
  4. Go again, smarterYour results shape the next exam. It spends more time on what you missed and changes the level as you improve.

Features

A personal tutor in your pocket.

Everything you need to learn a subject properly, without the clutter of a school portal.

Study anything

No fixed list of school subjects. Science, history, languages, professional exams or a personal goal all work the same way.

A lesson before each exam

Refresh the key ideas first. Try recall questions from memory, then tap to check the answer.

Exams written for you

Choose 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 or 50 questions, or your own number. Pick the level and question type, or leave it mixed.

Feedback that teaches

See the right answer and why after each question. Written answers are marked at the end, for meaning rather than exact wording.

Progress you can see

Track scores over time, mastery for each topic, weak topics and the ones showing improvement.

Pick up where you left off

Your exam is saved after every answer. Close the app and continue from Home whenever you are ready.

Exams that adapt

Every exam learns from the exam before it.

ExamMe looks at how you did across your recent exams, not just one answer. Then it plans the next exam around what will help most, and tells you why it made each choice.

  • More time on weak topicsTopics you struggle with get more questions next time
  • The right levelThe level goes up when you score well and eases off when you need it
  • Mastery takes more than one answerA topic counts as mastered only after strong results over several exams
  • New questions, same ideasYou get fresh questions on what you missed, not the same ones again
  • Your question count staysEach subject remembers how many questions you like

Privacy

Your study history stays on your phone.

ExamMe has no accounts. To write lessons and mark exams, it sends your subject, topics and written answers to an AI service, and nothing more than it needs. Read the full privacy policy.

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads
  • Your subjects, exams, scores and progress are stored on your phone
  • Your data is never sold
  • Everything sent to the AI service travels over an encrypted connection
  • Delete a subject or exam in the app, or uninstall to remove it all

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When can I download ExamMe?

ExamMe is in early testing on Google Play, for Android only. Become a tester to try it now. A public release will follow once testing is done.

How do I become a tester?

Send us the Gmail address you use on Google Play. We'll add you to the test and email you a link to install ExamMe. See Become a tester for the steps.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. ExamMe is Android only for now.

Who is ExamMe for?

Anyone aged 13 and over who wants to learn something: students, people studying for a work qualification, and anyone learning for fun.

Is the AI always right?

No. Lessons, questions and marks are written by AI and can contain mistakes. If a question looks wrong, report it in the app and it won't count toward your score. Check anything important against a trusted source.

Does it work offline?

Your subjects, exams and progress are saved on your phone, so you can look through them any time. Writing a new lesson and exam, and marking written answers automatically, need a connection. If you're offline, you can mark written answers yourself.

Is there a limit on exams?

ExamMe uses free AI services, so there is a daily limit. If it's reached, the app tells you when it will try again and carries on by itself while the screen is open.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Your study history lives on your phone, so uninstalling the app deletes it.
